## App introduction (up to 100 characters)

Tiered quantity discounts with a product-page picker. Buy more, save more, applied at checkout.

## App details (up to 500 characters)

Reward shoppers for buying in bulk. Set up quantity-break tiers such as
1 - 17 at full price, 18 - 35 at 5% off and 36+ at 20% off, and the app adds
a tier picker to your product page that sets the quantity in one click.
Discounts are applied automatically at checkout, with no codes. Choose
percentage off, an amount off each item or a set price per item, round prices
to .99, mark your most popular tier, and match the picker to your theme's
colours.

## Feature list (up to 80 characters each)

- Quantity-break tiers: % off, amount off each, or a set price per item
- Tier picker on the product page that updates the quantity for you
- Discount applied automatically at checkout, no codes needed
- Price rounding to .99, discount tags, ribbons and a running total
- Per-offer colours and layouts that match your theme

## Testing instructions for the reviewer

1. Install the app. It opens on the Offers page.
2. Click **Create offer**. Leave "All products" selected. The default tiers
   are Buy 2 for 10% off and Buy 3+ for 15% off. Click **Save**.
3. Click **Add block in theme editor** in the side panel, then save the theme.
   This adds the "Volume discount tiers" block to the product page.
4. Open any product on the storefront. Pick the 3+ card: the quantity changes
   to 3. Add to cart and go to checkout: 15% is taken off that product.
5. Back in the app, the switch on the Offers page pauses the offer, and the
   discount stops applying.

No test account or extra setup is needed. The app only needs the
`write_discounts` and `read_products` scopes.
